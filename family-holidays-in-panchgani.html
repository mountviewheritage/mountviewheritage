Family Holidays in Panchgani: Resorts for a Comfortable & Fun Stay

A family vacation is much more pleasant when there is something in the destination for all. Kids would need someplace interesting to play and explore while the parents usually prefer comfortable lodging, stunning location, and proximity to the nearby places. Panchgani combines all these characteristics through its mountainous location, views, activities, and relaxed atmosphere of a hill station.

For families planning a trip here, choosing the right accommodation can make a difference.The best Panchgani resorts for family holidays are not only about where you sleep. Location, room options, surroundings, and access to things to see and do are all worth considering before booking.

How to find the best family resort in Panchgani?

When travelling with children, there may be special considerations that must be taken into account in terms of accommodation. In addition to choosing a nice place, families need special amenities and enough space for comfortable staying with children.

Here are some things to consider when choosing a family resort in Panchgani:

  • Family-sized rooms: Large rooms will help parents spend time with their kids without any difficulty because everyone has enough space. Family rooms will be helpful for large families.
  • Children's play areas: This amenity allows providing children with a safe place where they can play and have fun between excursions.
  • Indoor and outdoor games: Table tennis, badminton, carrom and other leisure games are a good way to entertain children during the stay on a property.
  • Open gardens: It provides an opportunity to walk and move around and allows parents to rest while enjoying the view.
  • Good location: When choosing accommodation, families usually pay attention to their proximity to major sites and attraction of Panchgani.
  • Food options: Families often prefer accommodation where meals are convenient and children can find familiar choices.
  • Parking and basic conveniences: Easy parking, Wi-Fi, room service, and comfortable bathrooms can make a family stay more convenient.

These facilities are worth considering when comparing Panchgani hotels and resorts, especially for families planning a weekend or a longer holiday.

Things to Do in Panchgani with Kids

One of the biggest advantages of visiting Panchgani with children is that entertainment does not have to come from a theme park. The natural surroundings themselves offer plenty to explore.

  • Table Land is another top location that tourists prefer to visit. There are huge plateaus from which one can get vast views of the valleys and surrounding hills. Visitors have the chance to go for walks and engage in activities like horse riding. Trekking, paragliding, and exploring caves are just a few other activities that Maharashtra Tourism has identified in the area around Table Land.
  • Sydney Point is another convenient destination for a family that can easily be added to the itinerary. This destination provides visitors with the chance to view views of the Krishna Valley, Dhom Dam, and the hills around the area. Here is a good location for a family to relax outside and take pictures.
  • Parsi Point is yet another scenic spot that allows visitors to view valleys without necessarily taking several hours of their time.
  • Families visiting with kids who love food and activities should consider Mapro Garden as well.

Plan a Comfortable Family Day in Panchgani

Going around with kids does not necessarily entail covering all places on your itinerary at once. In fact, it might turn out better if you travel in a more leisurely fashion.

Families can begin with having breakfast at their hotel and then head to any of the viewpoints in Panchgani. After going through all these tourist places, one can return back to the hotel and get some rest before proceeding in the evening. The rest of the day can also be spent easily by visiting some other places nearby, or just going for a walk around, or simply admiring what is around. This leaves plenty of space in case kids get tired or the weather changes.

This is one of the reasons why when choosing Panchgani hotels and resorts, one should pay attention to the location and accommodation options.

Mount View Heritage Hotel for a Family Stay in Panchgani

Mount View Heritage Hotel is one of the best resort stay options for families looking for accommodation in Panchgani to consider. It provides heritage accommodation in the hills. It has 11 rooms in total and is situated in the Sahyadri Mountains at an elevation of 4,200 feet. There are rooms such as Deluxe Double, Deluxe Triple, Deluxe Quadruple, and Family Rooms at Mount View Heritage Hotel. The Family Room is able to provide accommodation for five people; thus, it can suit families traveling together. When considering Panchgani resorts for their holidays, parents would find heritage surroundings and various room types helpful.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Panchgani a good destination for a family holiday?
Yes. Panchgani offers scenic viewpoints, outdoor attractions, nature experiences, and family-friendly places to explore. Maharashtra Tourism lists attractions such as Table Land, Sydney Point, Parsi Point, and Mapro Garden.
2. What are some places to visit in Panchgani with kids?
Table Land, Sydney Point, Parsi Point, and Mapro Garden are some of the attractions families can consider.
3. What should families consider when choosing resorts in Panchgani?
Families should consider room capacity, location, access to attractions, and the type of stay that suits their travel plans.
4. Does Mount View Heritage Hotel have family room options?
Yes. The hotel's official website lists a Family Room and states that it accommodates up to five guests. It also lists Deluxe Double, Deluxe Triple, and Deluxe Quadruple rooms.
5. Is Mount View Heritage Hotel suitable for a family holiday in Panchgani?
The official website describes Mount View Heritage Hotel as suitable for family holidays and places the property in the Sahyadri Mountains, providing a base for exploring Panchgani.
